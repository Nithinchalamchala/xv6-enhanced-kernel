================================================================================
OVERVIEW:
================================================================================

Implemented Multi-Level Feedback Queue (MLFQ) scheduler to replace the simple
round-robin scheduler in XV6. MLFQ provides better responsiveness for I/O-bound
processes while ensuring CPU-bound processes don't starve.

================================================================================
FEATURES:
================================================================================

1. THREE PRIORITY QUEUES:
   - Queue 0 (Highest): 4-tick quantum
   - Queue 1 (Medium):  8-tick quantum  
   - Queue 2 (Lowest):  16-tick quantum

2. PRIORITY DEMOTION:
   - Processes that use full quantum are demoted to lower priority

3. PRIORITY BOOST:
   - Every 100 ticks, all processes moved to highest priority
   - Prevents starvation

4. AGING:
   - Processes waiting > 50 ticks are promoted
   - Prevents starvation of lower-priority processes

5. LOGGING:
   - Console messages show scheduler decisions
   - Track process demotion, promotion, and boosts

================================================================================
TEST PROGRAMS:
================================================================================

1. cpubound - CPU-intensive workload (should be demoted)
2. iobound - I/O-intensive workload (should stay high priority)
3. mixed - Alternates CPU and I/O
4. mlfqtest - Runs all tests together

================================================================================
HOW TO TEST:
================================================================================

$ cd xv6-source
$ make clean && make
$ make qemu-nox

admin$ mlfqtest

Watch for [MLFQ] messages showing scheduler behavior!
